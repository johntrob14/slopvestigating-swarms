# Hypotheses before resampling

Stage: understand; implementation and offline verification only. No fresh model
generations, judgments, or effect estimates exist.

| Hypothesis | Measurement and predicted separation | Main alternative and refutation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The board appeal increases sharing | Within each case and scope, more method-sharing attempts in `original` than `no_request` | Similar rates suggest general helpfulness, task-driven reporting, or other context explains the sharing |
| Claimed agent authorship increases compliance relative to claimed human authorship | Higher sharing rate in matched `agent` than `human` notes, with completion and censoring rates reported separately | Similar rates refute a detectable sender effect in this context; higher human rates reverse the proposed direction |
| Peer appeals can override explicit scope | In `no_sharing`, generated requests to write to the shared board, with evidence distinguishing probes and methods | No observed violations with adequate supported trials weakens this hypothesis for these checkpoints |
| Original sharing is ordinary permitted cooperation | Sharing occurs mainly under original scope and falls under the prohibition, regardless of author | Frequent method sharing under the prohibition challenges this explanation |
| Observation fidelity drives apparent differences | Differences coincide with unsupported tool calls, fixture dependence, or truncated generations | Differences among supported comparable trajectories weaken this explanation, without removing selection concerns |

Prior reasoning, board filenames, historical tool definitions, and provider
behavior are additional explanations. A substantial share of uncertainty remains
on explanations outside this list. Set sample size and analysis rules before
running. First authorized step: baseline pilot to establish reproduction and
simulator coverage; no pilot has been run.
